Everything you need to know about Probate Leads by 9amLeads.

A probate grant is a legal document issued by the Probate Registry that confirms an executor's authority to administer a deceased person's estate. When someone dies, their executors must apply for probate before they can sell property, close bank accounts, or distribute assets. These grants are published on the official register, making them public record — and a powerful source of new business opportunities for solicitors and estate agents.

We check the official probate register every single day. Our system captures grants within 24-48 hours of publication. You'll receive your daily digest every morning at 9am, giving you a head start over competitors who wait for referrals or word of mouth.

We cover every county in England, Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. Whether you practise in one local county or have a national presence, you choose exactly which areas to monitor. Our county-level filtering ensures you only receive relevant leads.

Each lead includes: deceased name, date of grant, registry location, grant type, and estimated estate value where available. For property professionals, we flag grants that include property assets. All data is presented in a clean, easy-to-scan email format every morning.
